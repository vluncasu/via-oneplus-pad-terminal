## Alternatives and reference envelopes

O05 0.40 mm is an optional replacement for the 0.20 mm default, not an extra required piece. R01 tablet, R02 supply, R03 USB plug and R04/R05 LED volumes are inspection references, not printable hardware.

White PETG: structural pieces and printed fasteners. Black PETG: P01, O01, O03 and O06. Natural PETG: O05. The present rigid feet are not TPU pads.

## Reading the file families

- B: base / stand and service enclosure.
- H: structural head and tablet retention.
- O: optical cassette, service cover, sensor face and film.
- P: one-piece dummy port cartridge.
- V: printed screw / foot instances.
- Q: optional fit and process probes.
- R: reference envelopes, never a print quantity.

Use [fasteners and service](fasteners-and-service.md) for the receiving interface of each V family, [manufacturing workflow](manufacturing-workflow.md) before large prints, and [assembly](assembly.md) for the build order.

This catalog is a file-selection aid. Its bounds and closed-mesh results do not qualify strength, fit, supports or optical performance.
